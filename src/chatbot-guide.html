🤖 Build Your Own Class Chatbot — Free, in About 30 Minutes

Make a chatbot like the Prompt Library assistant, but answering from your notes: FAQ, rubrics, scheme of work. Uses Dify and a free NVIDIA Build API key. No coding.

No coding Free key Works on phones
Home / References / Build Your Chatbot

✅ Before You Start

Dify is a free, no-code tool that turns your documents into a chatbot. NVIDIA Build gives you a free API key, which is the "brain" the chatbot uses to write answers. You need:

  • An email address (to sign up for NVIDIA Build and Dify).
  • 1 to 3 files of your own notes (a short FAQ, a marking rubric, a scheme of work). Text, Word, PDF or Markdown all work.
  • A laptop or computer for setup (the finished chatbot works on phones).
⚠️ Never upload student data. No names, IC numbers, marks, phone numbers or photos. Use general notes only, and follow your school's AI and PDPA policy.

Step 1 — Get a free NVIDIA Build key (5 min)

  1. Open build.nvidia.com and sign in Go to build.nvidia.com and create a free account. NVIDIA may ask you to verify a phone number.
  2. Pick a model Open the model list and choose a small, fast chat model. Open its page and click View Code (or "Get API Key"). Note the model ID shown after model=, for example nvidia/nemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b. Model names change over time, so always copy the ID from the page.
  3. Generate and copy your API key Click Generate API Key. It starts with nvapi- and is shown only once. Paste it into a private note. Never put this key in a web page, a chat or a screenshot.
💡 Limits: the free tier has a request limit and may change. Check the current limits on build.nvidia.com before you share your chatbot with a whole school.

Step 2 — Create your Dify account and connect NVIDIA (10 min)

  1. Sign up for Dify Cloud Go to cloud.dify.ai and sign up with your email or Google account. The free plan is enough to try this. See the current plan limits.
  2. Install the "OpenAI-API-compatible" plugin Open Plugins (top right), search for OpenAI-API-compatible and click Install. This lets Dify talk to any service that works like OpenAI, including NVIDIA.
  3. Add the NVIDIA model Click your profile picture, then Settings, then Model Provider. Under OpenAI-API-compatible choose Add Model and fill in the table below. Labels may look slightly different in your version.

Click Save. If Dify shows an error, see the troubleshooting table at the bottom.

Step 3 — Give the chatbot your notes (5 min)

  1. Create a Knowledge base Open Knowledge, click Create Knowledge, and upload your files.
  2. Choose the indexing settings Pick Economy (keyword search). It is free and needs no extra model. Keep the default chunk settings.
  3. Write your notes in small sections The chatbot finds one section at a time. Use short headings, one topic per section, for example ## How to submit homework followed by 3 to 5 lines. Clear notes give clear answers.

Step 4 — Create the chatbot (5 min)

  1. Create the app Go to Studio, click Create from Blank, choose Chatbot, and name it (e.g. "Form 4 Science Helper").
  2. Choose your model At the top right, select the NVIDIA model you added. Set Temperature to 0.3 (calmer, fewer made-up answers) and Max tokens to 1200. Some models "think" first, and a low limit can give empty replies.
  3. Attach your Knowledge Under Context, click Add and choose the Knowledge base from Step 3. Set Top K to 4.
  4. Paste the instructions Copy the template below into the Instructions box and fill in the [brackets].

Settings at a glance

SettingValueWhy
Temperature0.3Calmer answers, fewer made-up details

Top K (sections retrieved)4Enough context without confusing a small model
 
You are the class assistant for [SCHOOL / CLASS / SUBJECT].
Answer ONLY using the retrieved context (my notes).
If the answer is not in the context, say: "I can't find this in my notes. Please ask your teacher."
- Reply in the language of the student's latest message (English, Bahasa Melayu or 中文).
- Keep answers under 150 words, in simple words suitable for [Form / Year].
- Never invent facts, dates, marks or rules.
- If asked about something unrelated to [SUBJECT], reply politely: "I can only help with [SUBJECT] questions."
- Never ask for or store personal data (name, IC number, phone number).
💡 Why "ANSWER ONLY from my notes"? Small free models are good at quoting your notes but can guess when asked general facts. This one rule prevents most wrong answers.

Step 5 — Test it with 5 questions (5 min)

Use the preview panel on the right and ask:

  1. A question your notes answer clearly.
  2. The same question in Bahasa Melayu (or 中文). The reply should be in that language.
  3. A question your notes do not answer. It should say it can't find it.
  4. An unrelated question ("What is the capital of France?"). It should politely refuse.
  5. A question using different words from your notes. If it fails, add those words to your notes.

Step 6 — Share it

  1. Publish Click Publish, then Update. Every change to your notes or instructions needs a new publish.
  2. Share a link (easiest) Choose Run App and copy the web address. Send it to your class by WhatsApp or Google Classroom. It works on phones.
  3. Or add a chat bubble to a web page Choose Embed Into Site and copy the code into your Google Site, Blogger or school page, just before the end of the page. This is how the Prompt Library assistant works.
🔒 Safety checklist
  • Your nvapi- key stays inside Dify. It is never part of the embed code.
  • The link and bubble code are public: anyone who has them can chat and use up your free quota. Share it with your class only.
  • AI can be wrong. Check important answers (dates, marks, rules) against your notes.
  • If a key is ever pasted in a chat or page by mistake, delete it on build.nvidia.com and make a new one.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

What you seeLikely cause and fix
"404 page not found" when saving the modelWrong endpoint URL. Use exactly https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1.
"Model not found" or 404 on the model nameThe model ID is mistyped or has been renamed. Copy it again from the model page on build.nvidia.com.
401 or 403 errorThe API key is wrong or expired. Make a new nvapi- key.
429 "too many requests"Free limit reached. Wait a minute and try again, or share the bot with fewer people.

Replies in the wrong languageKeep the line "Reply in the language of the student's latest message" in the instructions.
"I can't find this" for things in your notesAdd the words students actually use as headings, and keep each section short.

💻 Backup: run Dify on your own computer

Dify is open source. If Dify Cloud does not fit your needs, you can run it with Docker on your own computer, fully free and private. It takes about 30 minutes and some comfort with the command line, so ask your school IT teacher to help. Follow the official guide at docs.dify.ai. Steps 1 to 6 above stay the same.
